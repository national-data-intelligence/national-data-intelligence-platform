## Marts (bi_* serving layer)

### mart_data_quality

Cross-source "source pulse" — data-quality signals at ONE grain: source × period × region × woreda, unioned across ALL SIX ingested sources plus the eLMIS historical archive (DHIS2, HCMIS, mBrana, EthioEMR, MFR, EWARS/Climate, eLMIS-hist). Sources report at their native geographic + temporal depth, so period_id and region_id are NULL where a source carries no such dimension (e.g. MFR is point-in-time reference data → period_id NULL; HCMIS/mBrana report at national grain → region_id NULL). completeness_pct/validity_pct/timeliness_pct are NULL where a source has no honest signal for that dimension yet. timeliness_pct is MEASURED for eLMIS-hist (real per-requisition submission times vs the governed deadline) and NULL for the sources that land as bulk snapshots. Source labels are clean, human-facing names (the raw '!'-prefixed record_source convention stays in the vault). Adapters must tolerate NULL dimensions. Consumed by /api/dq (source pulse + scorecard).

### mart_health_workforce

Region × cadre health-worker counts + WHO density per 10k, from the MFR facility staffing satellite (population from DHIS2 POP-TOTAL). Backs rm-workforce.

### mart_equipment_availability

National equipment inventory by type from the MFR FHIR Device satellite: working (active) / broken (inactive) / missing (entered-in-error) counts per equipment_type. Backs rm-equipment.

### bi_dhis2_facility_with_region

Every DHIS2 FAC-% facility org unit tagged with its level-2 (region) org_unit and its code-token tier.

### bi_facility_distribution

Internal view — counts per region per facility tier. Visible to R-DEC, R-CONS-ANA.

### bi_facility_distribution_public

Public k-anonymised view (k>=5) — collapsed across facility tiers.

### mart_facility_capacity

One row per DHIS2 FAC-% facility: tier token, region, MFR status/GPS, and the HCMIS reporting heartbeat (last stock-event date of the facility's HCMIS environment) → online/silent flag.

### mart_consent_activity_kpi

Governance KPI strip — exactly three (label, value) rows: "active consents" (gated patients the consent gate currently serves), "revoked (24h)", "pending review". active consents reuses the consent_gate() macro over the same population the PHI marts gate, so the KPI and the gate cannot drift apart.

### mart_dq_incidents

The durable DQ incident record on the governed mart path — one row per flag with events/acks counts and measured lifecycle latencies (hours_to_first_ack / hours_to_resolve are NULL until measured, never zero). Read-only reporting over the ops_governance FDW seam (40_dq_read.sql); the gateway remains the only writer.

### mart_dq_governance_kpi

Governance KPIs distilled from the incident history, long format (kpi_code, dim_a, dim_b, value_num), mirroring GET /api/dq/kpi so the two surfaces cannot drift. Steward KPIs stay API-only (the MDM read seam exposes just the decision log). Definitions: docs/data-quality-plane.md.

### mart_malaria_kpi

HLR Table 7 malaria MVP KPI set (region × month): suspected, tested by microscopy vs RDT, confirmed, test-positivity rate, confirmed per 1,000, inpatient deaths, ITN + IRS coverage — from the seeded MAL-* HMIS elements joined to POP-TOTAL. Backs dz-malaria-kpi.

### mart_epi_zero_dose

Immunization REACH INDEX × vaccine last-mile stress (region × year) — the HLR "zero-dose" question asked honestly at demo data scale: reach_index = annualized PENTA1 doses per 1,000 expected infants (POP-TOTAL × 3.2% CBR, labeled estimate) compares regions fairly; absolute zero-dose counts would mislead (demo-scale service volumes vs population-scale denominators). hotspot = bottom-tercile reach AND mBrana zero-stock ≥15%. Backs sv-zero-dose.

### mart_epi_cold_chain_safety

Month × national EPI safety surveillance — cold-chain temperature excursions + AEFI (mild/serious), from the DHIS2 monthly dataValues. Backs rm-cold-chain (honest 2-KPI safety strip).

### mart_hmis_indicator_coverage

HMIS reporting completeness at (region × woreda × programme × indicator × month). Source for the BI dashboard's coverage heatmap + the OPA "stale-source" alert.

### mart_public_summary

Public-tier HMIS summary aggregated to (region × programme × month). K-anonymity enforced upstream — every group surfaces facilities_reporting >= 5.

### mart_public_indicator_kpis

Public-tier indicator ACHIEVEMENT rate at (region × indicator × month) — the number the public KPI tiles, "coverage by region" bar and 24-month indicator trend show (Penta3/ANC4/SBA/mCPR coverage, TB success, ART suppression, U5 stunting). K-anonymity enforced in-SQL (every region cell has facilities_reporting >= 5). Which indicators are public, their labels, HSDIP targets and definitions come from public_indicator_reference; region names from public_region_reference — nothing hardcoded.

### mart_idsr_disease_weekly

PHEM/IDSR (Integrated Disease Surveillance & Response) weekly case + death counts per (region × woreda × disease × ISO-week). The fact table the EARS/outbreak anomaly models + the disease-surveillance widgets read from.

### mart_ml_disease_input

DENSIFIED woreda × disease × epi-week ML input frame for the disease forecast model family (ears/outbreak/seasonal/ewars_lite). Full grid of (surveillance woredas × priority diseases × every ISO week of the observed span) with cases/deaths 0-filled, region-grain climate broadcast onto each woreda ('ET-' org-unit prefix stripped at the join), and annual POP-TOTAL population LEFT JOINed by (woreda × year) — population stays NULL until the DHIS2 ingest carries the seeded values.

### mart_idsr_climate_drivers

Weekly disease signal cross-tabbed against rainfall/temperature anomalies (z-scores within region) with the strongest lagged cases↔rainfall correlation per region×disease. Populated from the climate source.

### mart_idsr_outbreaks

Outbreak EPISODES per (region × disease), derived from the weekly IDSR fact with an EARS-style threshold (mean + 2·SD of the preceding 8 weeks, per-disease minimum-case floor; consecutive flagged weeks collapse into one episode). Carries declared/last week, totals, CFR%, peak, and an active/contained status. The spine dz-outbreaks-table binds to; ML trajectory enrichment joins in when ops_ml.ml_predictions lands (#27).

### mdm_commodity_match_candidates

MDM discovered-relationship table: eLMIS v2 products ↔ entity-model commodities, top-3 trigram candidates per product (score ≥ 0.35), review_status pending. Never a same-as link until a steward accepts.

### mart_mdm_commodity_golden

Golden commodity record: entity-model commodity with per-source coverage (HCMIS, mBrana resolved; eLMIS candidates).

### mart_mdm_commodity_match_review

Steward review queue over the discovered candidates.

### mart_programme_cascade

Programme funnel attainment per (region × programme × year × step). Cascade steps are keyed on the REAL seeded DHIS2 element codes (HIV/TB/MNCH/NCD). value_total is measurement-kind aware (flow=annual sum, stock=avg of monthly totals, cohort_pct=pct × previous step — entity-model.yaml calc/unit is the authority); retention_pct_vs_entry is each step vs step 1. Monotonicity guarded by tests/cascade_monotonicity.sql.

### bi_facility_phcu_ratio

Phase-3.4 sample. Health-facility-to-PHCU ratio per region (PHCU anchors = health centers), derived from bi_facility_distribution. Lives in bi_staging until promoted to bi_facility via the gateway graduation flow.

### bi_demo_patient_observations

Phase-3.5 sample PHI mart. Demonstrates the consent_gate() macro — every row is from a patient_id with no active revocation for purpose=public-health. Lives in bi_staging.

### mart_supply_region_stock

Month × commodity × REGION months-on-hand (mos from catchment-blended soh/amc), from HCMIS hub stock events allocated through the epss_hub_catchment seed (hubs serve multi-region catchments — CE/SW have no hub of their own). Backs rm-stock-heatmap and the readiness leg of dz-region-map. Covers all 14 regions.

### mart_supply_stockout_events

One row per stockout EPISODE (contiguous zero-stock months for a HUB × commodity, gaps-and-islands over monthly SOH) × affected region (exploded through epss_hub_catchment — a region-scoped user sees the hub outages that threaten their supply; hub_id/hub_name carry the physical location). Backs rm-events. May be sparse when stock is healthy (honest empty).

### mart_supply_outbreak_response

"Act this week" — outbreak pressure (IDSR 4-week signal + trend) × response-commodity stock (catchment-blended latest-month mos), joined through disease_response_map. commodity NULL = no tracked countermeasure (honest absence). act_score ranks by cases × supply severity. Backs rm-act-week.

### mart_stockout_service_impact

Stockout cost in services per (region × commodity): days_out (12-month window) × the enabled indicator's avg monthly regional volume / 30. est_missed_services is DERIVED or NULL (no enables linkage), never guessed — consumers must label it an estimate. Backs rm-stockout-impact.

### mart_resource_allocation

Per (month × commodity) stock + consumption + wastage, NATIONAL — the HCMIS stock-event link has no facility FK, so there is no per-facility cut. Computes months-of-supply (mos) = soh_eom_avg / amc. Source for the stockout forecast model + the supply-chain dashboard (gateway fetchers treat it as national, geo_col=None).

### mart_supply_stockout_forecast

Per (facility-env × commodity) days-to-stockout projection using AMC-12. Status: stockout (≤0d), risk (1-14d), watch (15-30d), ok (>30d).

### mart_supply_stockout_public

K-anonymised public view — facility counts per region. n_facilities is the total reporting facilities (k>=5 enforced); n_stocked is how many had non-zero SOH; stocked_pct is the public-facing rate.

### mart_supply_pipeline

Vaccine procurement pipeline — one row per OPEN purchase order (HCMIS Vaccine.PurchaseOrder), with commodity name + destination-hub region and a LIVE eta_days (expected_delivery_date − CURRENT_DATE). Backs rm-pipeline.

### mart_ct_nodes

Every EPSS hub (warehouse) and delivery facility in the dispatch plan, one row each (node_kind = hub|facility). Reproduces plan.ts WAREHOUSES + NODES; region_id conforms the plan region name to the bi_supply 2-letter code for OPA geo scoping. Backs ct-movement / ct-dispatch (via /api/ct/plan).

### mart_ct_movements

One row per dispatch movement line (hub → facility × commodity × period), reproducing plan.ts MOVEMENTS. region_id enables geography scoping; programme_id conforms via commodity_reference; why_json holds the nested demand-signal breakdown. Backs ct-movement / ct-kpis.

### mart_ct_vehicle_runs

One row per dispatch vehicle run, reproducing plan.ts VEHICLE_RUNS. The per-run stop list + road polyline ride as JSON (stops_json / path_json). Backs ct-dispatch and the selected-run overlay on ct-movement.

### mart_ct_plan_stats

Per-period plan-level KPI + savings summary (units/legs/runs/facilities/ fill + km/cost/vehicles saved vs a naive one-truck-per-facility baseline). Reproduces plan.ts PERIOD_STATS and, for the current period, SUMMARY + BASELINE verbatim. Served as summary/baseline/periodStats by /api/ct/plan.

### mart_elmis_hist_monthly_activity

Legacy eLMIS v2 history — month × programme national supply activity (requisitions, dispensed/AMC, fill rate, stockout days) across 173 monthly periods from Jan 2013. Aggregated once from 7.5M R&R lines (table materialization). Added 2026-08-31.

### mart_elmis_hist_product_stockout

Legacy eLMIS v2 history — month × tracer product stockout pressure (stockout days, share of lines out of stock, dispensed volume).

### mart_elmis_hist_facility_activity

Legacy eLMIS v2 history — facility × programme × month rollup with zone context (≤74k rows). GOVERNED: facility identities derive from a restricted known-entity dump — internal display only pending data-owner clearance.

### mart_elmis_hist_availability

"Period availability" (the OSA-PROXY — never labeled OSA) per month × product set (tracer / ppe) × product: share of non-skipped reporting lines in stock with zero stockout days. PPE list pending user review.

### mart_elmis_hist_timeliness

Facility reporting timeliness per month × programme — on-time = requisition created within 15 days of period end (P1 baseline 79.8%).

### mart_elmis_hist_satp

Stock According To Plan (facility-grain ADAPTATION of the GF central- store measure) per month × programme: lines banded stocked_out / understocked / according / overstocked via amc and max_stock_quantity.

### mart_elmis_hist_facility_product_month

eLMIS v2 history — facility × product × month (non-skipped lines): the feature mart for elmis_consumption_forecast and the base of the resupply plan. Governed (facility codes).

### mart_elmis_hist_resupply_plan

eLMIS v2 history — planning-module mock: latest-month stock position, months of stock, suggested resupply quantity and SATP band per facility × tracer product.

### mart_pct_kpi

PCT KPI layer on the Zambia eLMIS archive — long format at base grain (KPI × month × province × programme | disease/category | band) with numerator and denominator on every row. No pre-aggregated rows: consumers compute sum(numerator) / sum(denominator) under the active filters (region_id = province code = profile region code; programme_id = eLMIS programme code; month_id 'YYYY-MM'). Contracts per KPI: seed pct_kpi_definitions. osa_s8 follows the Global Fund KPI Handbook S8 method with eLMIS period-end stock.

### mart_pct_facility_geo

PCT facility geography: every eLMIS facility located and labelled by how the point was obtained (coord_basis: source / repaired_lat_sign / repaired_lng_sign / district_facility_mean / province_facility_mean / none). region_id = profile region code (same geography filter as mart_pct_kpi). lat/lng are classified restricted / generalize.

### mart_pct_demand_month

PCT demand base — facility × product × programme × month (weekly-schedule reports collapsed; stock state from the period-end report). adjusted_demand = eLMIS's own normalized_consumption (dispensed where NULL; demand_basis says which); demand_censored = any stockout that month. pipeline_assumed is a labelled planning assumption (receipts are too sparsely recorded to measure it).

### mart_pct_position

PCT projected position — facility × tracer product × programme at the anchor month. AMC view (K4a, eLMIS max/min on AMC: risk_band, replenishment_*, redistribution) and planning view side by side (K4b, decision #28): planning_rate = the live champion forecast's P50 split by programme share, AMC for lumpy series, series without a forecast, or a forecast that is not the live champion (planning_basis says which); h1_risk = one-month stockout test (stock < P50 likely short; critical tracers stock < P90 at risk). Forecast snapshot on every row. No monetary value.

### mart_pct_forecast

PCT demand forecast (H1) per facility × tracer product from the latest contract-conforming run of elmis_consumption_forecast (P10/P50/P90, demand class, method), with provenance (model_version, predict run, MLflow run of the version's training) and geography from mart_pct_facility_geo.

### mart_pct_forecast_accuracy

Historical BACKTEST accuracy of the model version behind the current forecast (fold ledger written at training into ops_ml.model_performance): split × measure × subject (selected portfolio, each comparator incl. eLMIS AMC, each demand class). Never observed accuracy for the forecast months.
