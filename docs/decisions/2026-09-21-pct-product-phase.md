# Decision record — PCT product phase

Date: 2026-09-21 · Scope: the PCT decision-support build on the Zambia eLMIS archive
(KPIs, forecast surfacing, projected position, decisions, the PCT page).
Each decision lists its reason; reversals need a new entry here, not a silent edit.

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **On-shelf availability follows Global Fund KPI S8 exactly, with eLMIS as the source** (`mart_pct_kpi`, `kpi_code='osa_s8'`): per product category, facilities where ANY tracer of the category is in stock ÷ facilities where tracers of the category are expected; a disease score pools its categories; target 90%. Labelled "Global Fund KPI S8 method; eLMIS source (period-end stock)". | Method grounded in the KPI Handbook 2023-2028 (p.79-80) and its Dec-2024 amendments, which name eLMIS as the data source for countries with mature systems. The one departure — period-end stock instead of a visit-day observation — is stated on the definition row. |
| 2 | Tracer products map to the handbook's categories through a **reviewable seed** (`pct_gf_tracer_category`): 6 ARVs → HIV antiretrovirals; 2 HIV rapid tests → HIV diagnostics; the malaria RDT → Malaria diagnostics. | The archive has **no TB tracer** and **no malaria treatment tracer** (the only "mal" tracer is the RDT), so TB S8 is stated as not computable and malaria covers diagnostics only. A seed makes the judgement reviewable instead of buried in SQL. |
| 3 | "Expected to be available" = the facility submitted a non-skipped line for a tracer of the category that month. | eLMIS has no stocking-list table; reporting on the category is the observable evidence that the facility is meant to carry it. |
| 4 | **Base-grain long format**: one row per KPI × month × province × programme (or disease/category, or band), each with numerator and denominator; **no pre-aggregated national or disease rows**. | Consumers compute sum(numerator)/sum(denominator) under the active filters, so region, programme and period filters stay exact and nothing double-counts. Verified through the gateway's own filter builder. |
| 5 | Filter keys reuse the existing convention: `region_id` = eLMIS province zone code (identical to the Zambia profile's region codes), `programme_id` = eLMIS programme code, `month_id` 'YYYY-MM'. | No translation layer; the period window anchors on the latest month in the data, so "today" is August 2018 without `now()`. |
| 6 | `osa_s8` rows carry `programme_id = NULL`; **the S8 widget must declare no programme filter**. | S8 counts a facility once per category across programmes (ARVs are reported under two programmes). A programme filter would silently drop the rows — verified. |
| 7 | Weekly-schedule reports are **collapsed to one row per facility × product × month**: stock state from the latest report (period end); stockout occurrence = any report that month; quantities summed. | WEEKLY1 facilities submit several reports a month; without the collapse they were counted up to 5× and "available" meant "in any week" (found by the uniqueness test). |
| 8 | Order fill and stockout-day rates are **line-capped** (approved at the calculated quantity; stockout days at reporting days). | Approvals above the calculated quantity occur (up to 36×) and 2 lines report more stockout days than reporting days; uncapped, a rate can exceed 100%. Order fill is explicitly not OTIF. |
| 9 | SATP and reporting timeliness are **not recomputed** in the KPI mart; their existing marts stay the single definition, and `pct_kpi_definitions` documents them with those marts as the source. | One definition, one place. Their marts have no province breakdown, which the definition rows state. |
| 10 | PCT seeds are pinned to `bi_supply` (never the seeds' default `bi_public`); `mart_pct_kpi` is a table; both are in `mart_attrs` as PRD / DC.STK / aggregate. | The eLMIS archive is internal-only. Verified: R-DEC reads it, R-CONS-PRT / R-STEW / R-PUB-ANON are refused by OPA. New access rows take effect when the gateway reloads its catalogue — CI restarts the gateway on every security reseed. |
