# Decision record — PCT product phase

Date: 2026-09-21 · Scope: the PCT decision-support build on the Zambia eLMIS archive
(KPIs, forecast surfacing, projected position, decisions, the PCT page).
Each decision lists its reason; reversals need a new entry here, not a silent edit.

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **On-shelf availability follows Global Fund KPI S8 exactly, with eLMIS as the source** (`mart_pct_kpi`, `kpi_code='osa_s8'`): per product category, facilities where ANY tracer of the category is in stock ÷ facilities where tracers of the category are expected; a disease score pools its categories; target 90%. Labelled "Global Fund KPI S8 method; eLMIS source (period-end stock)". | Method grounded in the KPI Handbook 2023-2028 (p.79-80) and its Dec-2024 amendments, which name eLMIS as the data source for countries with mature systems. The one departure — period-end stock instead of a visit-day observation — is stated on the definition row. |
| 2 | Tracer products map to the handbook's categories through a **reviewable seed** (`pct_gf_tracer_category`): 6 ARVs → HIV antiretrovirals; 2 HIV rapid tests → HIV diagnostics; the malaria RDT → Malaria diagnostics. | The archive has **no TB tracer** and **no malaria treatment tracer** (the only "mal" tracer is the RDT), so TB S8 is stated as not computable and malaria covers diagnostics only. A seed makes the judgement reviewable instead of buried in SQL. |
| 3 | "Expected to be available" = the facility submitted a non-skipped line for a tracer of the category that month. | eLMIS has no stocking-list table; reporting on the category is the observable evidence that the facility is meant to carry it. |
| 4 | **Base-grain long format**: one row per KPI × month × province × programme (or disease/category, or band), each with numerator and denominator; **no pre-aggregated national or disease rows**. | Consumers compute sum(numerator)/sum(denominator) under the active filters, so region, programme and period filters stay exact and nothing double-counts. Verified through the gateway's own filter builder. |
| 5 | Filter keys reuse the existing convention: `region_id` = eLMIS province zone code (identical to the Zambia profile's region codes), `programme_id` = eLMIS programme code, `month_id` 'YYYY-MM'. | No translation layer; the period window anchors on the latest month in the data, so "today" is August 2018 without `now()`. |
| 6 | `osa_s8` rows carry `programme_id = NULL`; **the S8 widget must declare no programme filter**. | S8 counts a facility once per category across programmes (ARVs are reported under two programmes). A programme filter would silently drop the rows — verified. |
| 7 | *(rationale corrected — see #14)* Weekly-schedule reports are **collapsed to one row per facility × product × month**: stock state from the latest report (period end); stockout occurrence = any report that month; quantities summed. | WEEKLY1 facilities submit several reports a month; without the collapse they were counted up to 5× and "available" meant "in any week" (found by the uniqueness test). |
| 8 | Order fill and stockout-day rates are **line-capped** (approved at the calculated quantity; stockout days at reporting days). | Approvals above the calculated quantity occur (up to 36×) and 2 lines report more stockout days than reporting days; uncapped, a rate can exceed 100%. Order fill is explicitly not OTIF. |
| 9 | SATP and reporting timeliness are **not recomputed** in the KPI mart; their existing marts stay the single definition, and `pct_kpi_definitions` documents them with those marts as the source. | One definition, one place. Their marts have no province breakdown, which the definition rows state. |
| 10 | PCT seeds are pinned to `bi_supply` (never the seeds' default `bi_public`); `mart_pct_kpi` is a table; both are in `mart_attrs` as PRD / DC.STK / aggregate. | The eLMIS archive is internal-only. Verified: R-DEC reads it, R-CONS-PRT / R-STEW / R-PUB-ANON are refused by OPA. New access rows take effect when the gateway reloads its catalogue — CI restarts the gateway on every security reseed. |
| 11 | **Facility geography** (`mart_pct_facility_geo`) locates every eLMIS facility and labels how: `source`, `repaired_lat_sign` (13), `repaired_lng_sign` (1), `district_facility_mean` (inferred), `province_facility_mean`, `none`. Out-of-country points a single sign flip does not recover (7) are rejected, never guessed. | Zambia lies entirely in the southern hemisphere and east of Greenwich, so a positive latitude or negative longitude inside the mirrored box is a dropped minus sign — the 14 Bini's profile noted. Every repair is recomputed exactly from the reported point. |
| 12 | The fallback for facilities without a usable point is the **mean of their district's usable facility points**, then the province's. | eLMIS districts carry no coordinates of their own (0/108), so the planned "district centroid" did not exist. 733 of 2,393 reporting facilities (31%) are inferred — **the map must style inferred points distinctly**. |
| 13 | Facility `lat`/`lng` are classified `restricted` / `generalize` **before the data lands**, the same policy as `mart_ct_nodes`. | The house classification rule. Verified: R-DEC and R-CONS-ANA see exact points; the same analyst on a low-trust login sees them generalized to one decimal; R-CONS-PRT is refused. |
| 14 | **Correction to #7.** The source feature mart is already one row per facility × product × programme × month (verified: no key carries a second report), so no facility was "counted up to 5×". The duplicate rows the uniqueness test caught came from grouping on the source's `period_start`, which for some programmes starts mid-month and split one calendar month into several groups. The fix — key on `month_id` — stands; K1's numbers were never inflated (full-table regression after the refactor: identical). The collapse logic now lives in `mart_pct_demand_month` and is defensive only. | Recorded as a new entry, per this record's rule, rather than silently rewriting #7. |
| 15 | **Adjusted demand = eLMIS's own `normalized_consumption`** (`mart_pct_demand_month.adjusted_demand`), with dispensed quantity only where it is NULL (`demand_basis`); months with any stockout are flagged `demand_censored`. | The source system already computes stockout-adjusted consumption and builds its AMC from it; re-deriving a textbook version (which matches it on only 42% of stockout lines) would create a second, conflicting definition. |
| 16 | **Pipeline is a labelled planning assumption**, not a measurement: `pipeline_assumed` = the month's approved quantity, treated as in transit. The old plan's "approved − received" is dropped. | Receipts are recorded on only 13% of tracer lines, and approval-to-receipt agreement peaks at 23% (2-month lag) — the archive cannot measure the pipeline. The position mart shows results with and without the assumption. |
| 17 | **Projected position is AMC-based and descriptive** (`mart_pct_position`, `demand_basis = elmis_amc`) until the forecast contract, backtest and promotion gates pass; then only `demand_basis` changes. Policy is explicit per row: target = max months × AMC, emergency level = EOP × AMC, lead time and review period from `pct_policy_parameters` (lead time 30 days = ASSUMPTION from the Global Fund calibration). Replenishment is shown excluding (primary, conservative) and including the assumed pipeline. | Head of Data Science review (2026-09-21): forecast-driven decisions need a verified contract first. Recomputed by hand for three risk bands and one district's redistribution; 49% of anchor-month tracer rows stocked out, against 52% in the Global Fund Zambia calibration (Mar-2022). |
| 18 | **eLMIS AMC is the only AMC** — rows without it are flagged (`flag_amc_missing`, 266), not filled; `flag_no_recent_demand` (6,459) separates true zero demand. | A trailing-3 AMC agrees with eLMIS's on only 56% — deriving one would create a second definition. |
| 19 | **No monetary value** in the position mart. | eLMIS `current_price` mixes per-test, per-bottle and bottle-labelled-"each" semantics with no currency; a value column could be off 30-100×. Open question for the data owner. |
| 20 | **`elmis_consumption_forecast` v2 follows the forecast contract**: six comparators on an expanding-window fold ledger (H1), method chosen per demand class, empirical P10/P50/P90, contract keys in `attrs`, Gaussian band retired. The backtest selected **trailing 3-month mean** (smooth, erratic, intermittent) and **TSB** (lumpy); **damped Holt — the v1 model — was not selected for any class**. | Evidence decides the method, not its reputation. Holdout (Aug 2018): WAPE 0.598 vs naive-last 0.630, trailing-3 0.616, eLMIS AMC 0.627; bias −3.0%; P10–P90 coverage 81% (nominal 80%); predeclared champion rule passed. |
| 21 | **The ML runner logs a model's own `metrics` and `evaluation` inside the registered run** (generic, optional attributes; models without them unchanged), tags the feature-query hash, and carries `eda/` + `forecast_contract.yaml` with the package. | The v1 "MASE 0.977" was never logged because the runner opened the run after training; a metric that exists only in job output does not exist. |
| 22 | **New versions register as `challenger`**; `champion` moves only by the contract's promotion rule (M3). v2 of the registry was a pre-documentation run (stale card) and carries no alias; v3 is the challenger. | `runner train` defaults to `--alias champion` and `seed-ml.sh` passes no alias, so every seeded retrain silently promoted — a platform gap recorded for the platform owner. |
| 23 | **Forecasts reach marts through a read seam, `nhdw_vault_ml`** (init `41_ml_read.sql`): `ml_predictions`, `ml_run` and `model_performance` imported read-only from `ops_ml` over the existing `nhdw_ops_ro` server — the same pattern as `nhdw_vault_governance` (init 40). | dbt builds in `nhdw_vault` and cannot read `nhdw_ops`; a second copy of predictions would drift. Idempotent; cold-boot initdb test green. |
| 24 | **The runner writes a model's backtest into `ops_ml.model_performance`** when the package exposes `performance_rows` (one row per fold window, metrics named `backtest_*`, linked to the training `ml_run`). v4 supersedes v3 as the challenger for this reason alone — same code path, same result (holdout WAPE 0.598). | Accuracy on a surface must come from the evidence the registered version was selected on, queryable in SQL, never retyped. `backtest_*` names keep it distinct from live scoring. |
| 25 | **`mart_pct_forecast` reads only the latest batch run of a contract version** (a version with `backtest_*` rows); pre-contract versions — including the daily v1 champion batch — are ignored. The run is chosen from the small run tables and predictions are filtered by a scalar `run_id`, which postgres_fdw pushes to ops. | A join shipped all 6.2M prediction rows across the seam (522 s); the scalar filter fetches only the run (0.6 s). Provenance on every row: model version, predict run, MLflow training run. Champion status is MLflow's, read by the surface. |
| 26 | **Pooled RMSSE is not surfaced.** It is logged (holdout 23.5) but averages squared-error ratios across series, so near-zero-scale series dominate; WAPE, bias, coverage and MAE are the accuracy shown. | A metric that cannot be read correctly is not shown until its aggregation is per-series (open item for the model). |
| 27 | **`elmis_consumption_forecast` v4 is the champion** (2026-09-21), promoted through the existing gateway route by an operator (R-OPS-ADM): MLflow alias + gateway audit event. v1 stays registered for rollback. The v2 package is in the object store at `ml-packages/elmis_consumption_forecast/{v2,current}/`; v1 archived at `…/v1/`. | Contract §6 rule passed in v4's own run (tuning 0.573 and holdout 0.598 beat naive-last and trailing-3; bias −3.0%; coverage 81%). Truthful limits: the route does not check the rule, promotion does not write `promotion_log`, and a drift-triggered retrain would still take the champion alias without the rule — platform gaps for the owner. |
| 28 | **Forecast-driven position** (`mart_pct_position`, planning view beside the AMC view): order sizes use the live champion's **P50** as the monthly rate in eLMIS's max/min policy, split across programmes by their share of recent demand; **lumpy series, series without a forecast and non-champion forecasts stay on AMC**, labelled in `planning_basis`. **P90 is a one-month risk test for critical tracers** (`h1_risk`), not a sizing rate. "Live champion" = the model version of the latest scheduled batch run. Clarifies contract §8. | Measured on the Aug 2018 holdout (5,842 series): P50 volume bias −3.1%, WAPE 0.597 (AMC +8.6%, 0.627), but lumpy −45%; P90 as a rate +128%.
